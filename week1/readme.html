"Creative Coding Is..."

Creative Coding is the practice of applying code, a rhythmic and technological means of communication, to create, manipulate, use, and share art. While code's usecase and applications are oftentimes belittled to that of having scientific applications alone, this is not the case. In the following quotes, from myself and from other creative coders online, a broad understanding of the medium that is creative coding can be gathered.
