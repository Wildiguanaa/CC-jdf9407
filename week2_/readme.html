5 Iterations by Joanna

With each iteration/step/frame, I made the following adjustments: 1. Square 1 is placed on the lower left quadrant of my canvas, triangle 1 situated on the upper right quadrant, and circle 1 designated on the center right quadrant. 2. Square 2 is moved down near the bottom edge of the canvas, triangle 2 is rotated in the upper-left quadrant, and circle 2 is made slightly larger in the center-right quadrant. 3. Square 3 is moved forward toward the bottom-center and rotated, triangle 3 is shifted to the top-center and rotated, and circle 3 is made larger in the center-right quadrant. 4. Square 4 is scaled down to half its size near the bottom edge, triangle 4 is scaled up to three times its size at the top-center while rotated, and circle 4 is moved straight up to the top edge of the center-right section. 5. Square 5 is centered and rotated in the middle of the canvas, triangle 5 is scaled down to half its size with its rotation reset at the top-center, and circle 5 is shrunk back to size 30 and placed in the lower-center quadrant.
